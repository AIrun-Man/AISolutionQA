# Review Standards

Reviewers evaluate correctness, architecture, testability, maintainability, security, privacy, and operational impact.

| Risk | Required approvals |
|---|---:|
| Low | 1 qualified reviewer |
| Medium | 2 qualified reviewers |
| High | 2 reviewers including an affected-area owner |
| Critical | 3 reviewers including architecture and affected-area owners |

Blocking and required comments must be resolved. Material changes invalidate approvals. Required checks, traceability, validation evidence, and documentation must be complete before merge.
